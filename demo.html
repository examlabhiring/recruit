Exam Lab Invigilator & System Operator

Computer-Based Examinations

Join our professional team overseeing secure, computer-based exam labs. We are hiring invigilators to monitor candidates and system operators to manage technical infrastructure.

What You'll Do

Job Responsibilities

🪪

Verify Candidate Identities

Thoroughly check and verify the identity of each candidate before they enter the examination lab.

👁️

Monitor During Exams

Actively monitor candidates throughout the exam session to prevent malpractice and ensure focus.

📜

Enforce Exam Rules

Ensure all exam rules and guidelines are strictly followed by every candidate in the lab.

🛡️

Maintain Discipline

Uphold proper invigilation procedures and maintain a disciplined, quiet exam environment.

🖥️

Set Up Exam Systems

Install and configure computer systems, software, and peripherals before exam sessions.

🔧

Troubleshoot Technical Issues

Quickly resolve hardware/software glitches during exams to avoid disruptions.

🌐

Manage Network & Connectivity

Ensure stable internet and LAN connectivity for all exam terminals.

🔐

Maintain Exam Security

Manage login credentials and prevent unauthorized access.
